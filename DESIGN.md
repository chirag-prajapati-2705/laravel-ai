# Design System: Synapse Pro

## Creative North Star: "Hyper-Spatial Clarity"
The interface is designed to feel like a series of translucent, illuminated layers floating in a deep, cosmic void. It prioritizes depth, translucency, and intentional asymmetry over rigid boxes and solid borders.

## Typography
Authoritative display paired with precision utility.

- **Headlines (Manrope)**: Authorship and editorial voice. Slightly tight tracking (-0.02em).
- **Body & Labels (Inter)**: Functional voice for clarity and precision.

---

## UI Philosophy: The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
- **Glassmorphism**: Floating elements (navigation, modals) use `surface-variant` (`#2d3449`) at 60-80% opacity with a `12px` to `20px` backdrop blur.
- **No-Line Rule**: Avoid 1px solid borders. Define sections through color shifts, subtle gradients, and negative space.
- **Elevation**: Depth is achieved through layer stacking and ambient, environmental shadows (not structural).
- **Buttons**: Primary buttons use a 135-degree gradient from `primary` to `primary-container`.
